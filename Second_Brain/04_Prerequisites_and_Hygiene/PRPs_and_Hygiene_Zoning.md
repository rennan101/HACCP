# Prerequisite Programmes (PRPs) & Hygiene Zoning

## Overview
Prerequisite Programmes ([[What_is_HACCP|PRPs]]) provide the essential environmental and operational conditions necessary for the production of safe food. Without robust PRPs, [[7_HACCP_Principles|HACCP Critical Control Points]] cannot function effectively.

---

## The 7 Foundational PRP Pillars
1. **Cleaning & CIP Sanitation**: Validated automated wash cycles, chemical titration logs, potable water rinses.
2. **Personal Hygiene & PPE**: Clean workwear, captive footwear, hairnets, beard snoods, and strict illness reporting.
3. **Pest Control**: External tamper-proof bait boxes, internal electric fly killers (EFKs), routine contractor inspections.
4. **Maintenance & Calibration**: Planned Preventative Maintenance (PPM), food-grade lubricants, probe calibrations.
5. **Supplier Assurance**: Sourcing raw milk and ingredients exclusively from BRCGS/FSSC-certified suppliers.
6. **Allergen Management**: Strict segregation, dedicated scoops, and validated rapid protein swab testing.
7. **Staff Training & Competency**: Ongoing statutory food safety qualifications and refresher assessments.

## 3-Tier Factory Zoning & Stationery Coding

| Zone | Area Type | PPE & Access Barrier | Stationery Colour |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High-Risk (Aseptic)** | Open ready-to-eat dairy filling | Captive white boots, overcoats, positive air pressure | **Green Pen** |
| **High-Care** | Pasteurised cheese curds & yogurt vats | Bootwash barrier, dedicated utensils, hair containment | **Red Pen** |
| **Low-Risk / Intake** | Raw tanker intake deck & warehouse | Standard safety boots, outer packaging storage | **Blue Pen** |
